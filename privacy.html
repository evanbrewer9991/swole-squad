Privacy policy

Last updated: November 1, 2026

Short version: Swole Squad keeps your information on your iPhone. We don't run servers and there are no accounts. We don't collect, sell or share your data.

What the app stores, and where

Everything stays on your iPhone, in the app's own protected storage: your avatar's name, look and progress; the workouts you log; GPS routes from runs you record (location is used only between Start and Finish of a run you record, including while the phone is locked during that run); routine cover photos you choose; and app settings.

If you add the Home Screen widget, the app keeps a small summary for it on your iPhone: your avatar's name, level, picture and this week's workouts. The rest timer between sets can show on your Lock Screen; it runs on your iPhone only.

What leaves your iPhone

Nothing is sent to us. We have no servers or analytics, and no advertising or tracking.

Route maps are drawn with Apple Maps. Optional Coin pack purchases go through Apple's App Store; Apple processes payment, and we never see your payment details. Apple's own privacy policy covers these.

If you test a beta through Apple's TestFlight app, Apple may send us crash reports, and feedback and screenshots you choose to send. We use them only to fix the app.

If you tap Share on a workout, garden or evolution card, the image goes only where you send it. It contains no location or account details.

Health and safety

Swole Squad isn't a medical app and doesn't read Apple Health. Exercise at your own pace, and stop if you feel pain or unwell.

Your choices

Location is allowed only "While Using the App", and you can turn it off in iOS Settings at any time. Settings → Delete all my data removes your avatar, workouts, routes, Coins (including purchased Coins), items, photos and the widget's summary from your iPhone. Deleting the app also removes them.

Children

Swole Squad is not directed at children under 13, and it collects no personal information from anyone.
