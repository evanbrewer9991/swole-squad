A cozy fitness game for iPhone

Train for real.
Grow your Bear.

Every set you log gives your Bear XP. The way you train decides how it evolves: strength, speed, endurance or control.

Now in beta on TestFlight. Coming to the App Store.

Train

Log lifting and bodyweight sets in seconds, follow a guided walk–jog, or record a run. A rest timer sits on your Lock Screen between sets.

Play

Walk the Lantern Grove trail. Each stop opens when you've trained enough. Battle trail creatures in turn-based fights where your choices matter.

Grow

Plant a garden that blooms as you keep showing up, and decorate your clubhouse with things you earn.

Fair by design

  • Everything is earned by training. Coins only buy looks, never power.
  • No ads, no account, no tracking.
  • Your workouts stay on your iPhone.

Swole Squad isn't a medical app. Talk to a doctor before starting a new exercise program.
