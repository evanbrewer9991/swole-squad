Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email evanbrewer24@gmail.com and include your app version (Settings → About).

Common questions

Where is my data? On your iPhone, and in your own iPhone or iCloud backup if you use one. There's no account, so to move to a new phone, restore your backup.

Do I have to pay? No. Everything can be earned by training. Coin packs are optional and only buy looks, never power.

I bought Coins and they're missing. Close and reopen the app, then check the Shop. If they still haven't arrived, email us with the date of purchase. Coins move with your iPhone or iCloud backup. Coins bought after your latest backup, or with no backup at all, can't be restored, because Coin packs are used up when spent.

Why did a workout give no Coins? Coins come from your first two rewarded workouts each day. Every workout still gives XP and grows your Bear and garden.

How do mystery packs work? Each pack shows its odds of a common, rare or legendary item before you open it. Bigger packs have better odds. You never get an item you already own, and every item is just for looks.

Can I get a refund? Refunds for Coin packs are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. When a refund goes through, the refunded Coins are removed from your balance (never below zero). Items you already got from packs stay yours.

How do I delete everything? Settings → Delete all my data.
