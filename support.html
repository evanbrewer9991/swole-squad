Support

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email evanbrewer24@gmail.com and include your app version (Settings → About).

Common questions

Where is my data? On your iPhone only. There's no account, so a new phone starts fresh unless you restore an iPhone backup.

Do I have to pay? No. Everything can be earned by training. Coin packs are optional and only buy looks, never power.

I bought Coins and they're missing. Close and reopen the app, then check the Shop. If they still haven't arrived, email us with the date of purchase. Coin packs are used up when spent, so they can't be restored on a new device.

How do I delete everything? Settings → Delete all my data.

Testing the beta? Take a screenshot in the app and tap Share Beta Feedback, or open TestFlight and tap Send Beta Feedback.
